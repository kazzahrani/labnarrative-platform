Research

How we investigate the p53 isoform network

Our research connects molecular mechanisms with cell fate, tumour behaviour, patient stratification and therapeutic translation.

01

The p53 isoform network

We study TP53 as a multi-protein system. Alternative promoters, translation starts and RNA splicing generate distinct p53 isoforms that combine to shape cellular responses to stress.

How are individual p53 isoforms produced, regulated and assembled into a functional network?

TP53 locus · isoform architecture
02

Cell fate and adaptive homeostasis

We investigate how p53-isoform balance influences apoptosis, senescence, proliferation, DNA repair and tissue regeneration.

How does isoform balance translate cellular stress into distinct cell-fate decisions?

Stress · repair · senescence · survival
03

Cancer behaviour and patient stratification

We examine how altered p53-isoform expression affects tumour invasion, treatment response and patient outcome.

Can p53-isoform profiles explain tumour invasion, treatment response and patient outcome?

04

Diagnostics and therapeutic translation

We develop isoform-specific tools and apply them to cancer predisposition, biomarker development and therapeutic strategies.

How can isoform-specific tools improve diagnosis, disease modelling and therapeutic design?
