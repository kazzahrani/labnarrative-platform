Bourdon Lab Website

Prepared for Dr Jean-Christophe BourdonUniversity of Dundee · Cancer Research · School of Medicine

Scope of work

  • Refine and complete the existing Bourdon Lab website concept, including Research, Publications, Team, Opportunities and Contact.
  • Add an interactive questions area for students, young researchers and companies.
  • Add a basic private p53 Learn Society discussion area.
  • Add a Reagents section for antibodies and primers.
  • Add a Blog section for detailed scientific articles and explanations.
  • Add an announcements / relevant promotional content area.
  • Complete responsive, content and rendering quality assurance before launch.

Deliverables

  • Completed and launched Bourdon Lab website based on the approved concept.
  • Responsive desktop and mobile presentation.
  • Questions, Reagents, Blog and announcements sections.
  • Managed website hosting, SSL and technical monitoring for the first 12 months, with UK-region website hosting configured in line with the client's preference.
  • Editable LabNarrative website with revision history and final client handover.
  • LabNarrative Annual Care for the first 12 months after launch.
