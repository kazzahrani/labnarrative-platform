# LabNarrative Systems — Sales & Product Direction

## Core direction

LabNarrative Systems uses a discovery-led, vertical-first sales model.

Initial market focus:
- Saudi/GCC scientific distributors
- laboratory suppliers
- diagnostic and medical distributors
- technical/healthcare B2B suppliers
- adjacent operationally complex businesses

Primary positioning:

> Fast, modern operational systems built around how the company actually works.

Do not lead with generic AI or CRM positioning. AI is embedded where it improves real execution. LabNarrative Systems may work alongside Odoo, Zoho, ERP/accounting and other existing systems rather than requiring replacement.

## Validated distributor operating model

Default workflow:

**Tender / Enquiry → Quotation → Order → Warehouse → Supply → Invoice → Collection → Management Overview**

Supporting capabilities:
- Customers
- Tasks / documents
- Automation
- AI assistance
- Integrations
- Team / permissions

Technical/application review is normally a gate inside tender/quotation flow rather than a primary standalone module.

## Two-audience product design

### Operational user
The system should answer:
- What is due?
- What is missing?
- What is blocked?
- Is every requested line item complete?
- What needs follow-up?
- Which invoice is overdue?

### Management
The Overview should answer:
- What is active?
- What is at risk?
- What is delayed?
- What is outstanding?
- What is collectible?
- Which team/process needs attention?

Management Overview is a buying feature, not a cosmetic dashboard.

## AI policy

Avoid making a generic AI Command Center the main value proposition.

Prefer embedded operational AI such as:
- missing quotation line alert
- tender deadline risk
- stock shortage linked to an order
- incomplete shipment block
- overdue invoice prioritization
- collection next action
- management risk summary

AI should primarily answer: **What needs attention now?**

## Acquisition funnel

**Research → tailored teaser → human outreach → reply → discovery → Demo V2 → internal review → paid pilot → full implementation → integrations → multi-company expansion**

The first demo is intentionally a teaser. Do not overbuild or invent internal processes before speaking to the company.

After a reply, stop generic selling and enter discovery.

## Discovery framework

Key questions:
1. What 2–3 activities take the most time or require the most manual follow-up?
2. Where do omissions or operational errors happen?
3. What is still tracked in Excel, WhatsApp or email?
4. What does management struggle to see quickly?
5. Which systems are already used?
6. What should remain inside Odoo/Zoho/ERP, and what is missing between those systems?
7. Which single workflow would make the strongest paid pilot?

## Contact strategy

Do not target CEOs only.

Prioritize a combination of:
- Operations
- Tenders
- Commercial / Sales
- Supply Chain
- Procurement
- Warehouse
- Finance / Collection
- Business Development
- General Manager / Owner / CEO
- Technical / Applications leadership

Aim to identify:
- an operational internal champion
- a management/economic buyer
- a relevant technical/commercial stakeholder

## Demo strategy

Use approximately:
- **80% shared master operating model**
- **20% prospect-specific context**

The teaser should be good enough to earn a conversation, not pretend to be the final company system.

After discovery, Demo V2 should use the prospect's own confirmed pain points and language.

All fabricated operational records must be clearly illustrative. Never imply access to internal company data.

## Pilot model

Default initial offer:

**Operational Pilot — SAR 7,500**

The pilot is a real usable implementation of one high-value workflow. It is not the final enterprise-wide system.

The full system is scoped separately after discovery/pilot validation and may include:
- integrations
- additional modules
- users and permissions
- branches
- warehouses
- advanced automation
- training
- reporting
- multi-company architecture

## Multi-company opportunity

Support future architecture where a group can have:
- Group Overview
- Company A workspace
- Company B workspace
- Factory / business-unit workspace
- separate permissions/data where required
- consolidated ownership/management reporting

This is a premium expansion path, not automatically part of the small pilot.

## Product learning loop

Every real prospect conversation should become durable sales/product intelligence.

Track recurring:
- pain points
- current software
- workflow gaps
- management KPIs
- integrations
- objections
- buying triggers
- pilot candidates

After several customers in the same vertical, use the common 80% to evolve the custom-service model into a repeatable vertical platform.
